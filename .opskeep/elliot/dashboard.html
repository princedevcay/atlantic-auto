1. Overview

The constraint

Prospects cannot start a USA import quote online, and existing customers have no clear way to ask where their vehicle is, so both land as ad hoc calls and WhatsApp messages that are slow to answer and easy to lose.

Evidence: Owner's description of current enquiries (project gate assumption AS-07); no measured figures yet.

The bet

We believe customers will use simple online forms and a private tracking link if they are quicker than calling and do not expose their details, so we are betting several weeks of build effort on existing shared hosting, with no new paid security services that this produces more import quote requests captured and vehicle update requests that arrive complete and get answered faster, without a data leak; we will watch quote requests per week, share of update requests with a VIN or reference, and zero unauthorised tracking page views.

Why MEDIUM

  • Staff authentication and an internet-facing admin area
  • Customer personal data (names, phones, emails) and vehicle identifiers (VINs)
  • Customer-facing tracking pages that must not reveal another customer's shipment
  • Third-party carrier API, email delivery and file uploads by staff
  • No payments, no AI or agents, no multi-tenant SaaS, so not HIGH

Outcomes

  • More import quote requests are captured
  • Vehicle update requests arrive complete and are answered faster
  • Customers can see their own vehicle's stage, and nobody else's
  • Staff accounts and customer data stay out of attackers' hands

Rejected paths
